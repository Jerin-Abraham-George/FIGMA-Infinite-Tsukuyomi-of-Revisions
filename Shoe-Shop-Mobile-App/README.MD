# 👟 STEPUP – Shoe Shop Mobile App UI/UX Design (Figma)

## 📘 Overview
**STEPUP** is a modern **shoe shopping mobile app UI/UX design** created in **Figma**, focused on delivering a smooth and visually engaging e-commerce experience for footwear lovers.

The project covers the complete shopping journey — from splash onboarding and product discovery to detailed product customization, cart management, and order confirmation.

## 🎯 Objectives
- To design a **real-world e-commerce mobile shopping experience**  
- To practice **product-focused UI and visual hierarchy**  
- To create an intuitive **shopping → checkout → confirmation flow**  
- To demonstrate **component-based, developer-ready UI design**

---

## 🔑 Key Features
- **Splash / Onboarding Screen:**  
  - Brand-first introduction with strong visual storytelling  
  - Clear CTA to enter the app  

- **Home Screen:**  
  - Product search bar  
  - Category filters (Lifestyle, Running, Football, etc.)  
  - Best seller product grid  
  - Add-to-cart controls directly on product cards  

- **Product Details Screen:**  
  - Large product preview with carousel-style interaction  
  - Product ratings and reviews  
  - Color selection with visual indicators  
  - Shoe size selection  
  - Clear pricing and confirmation CTA  

- **Cart & Checkout:**  
  - Cart item list with quantity controls  
  - Dynamic total price calculation  
  - Empty cart state  
  - Confirm payment CTA  

- **Order Success Screen:**  
  - Visual confirmation of successful purchase  
  - Friendly messaging and return-to-home action
