# ✈️ SKYLANE – Flight Booking Mobile App UI/UX

## 📘 Overview
**SKYLANE** is a mobile flight booking application UI/UX project designed in **Figma**, created to provide a seamless experience for discovering destinations, searching flights, managing passengers, making payments, and completing bookings.

The project includes a structured **design system, reusable components, wireframes, high-fidelity screens, and interactive prototype transitions**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- To design a complete **end-to-end flight booking experience**.
- To create a consistent and reusable **mobile design system**.
- To simplify flight discovery, selection, and booking.
- To explore interactive prototyping and animation in Figma.

---

## 🔑 Key Features
- **Flight Discovery:** Explore popular, favorite, and frequently visited destinations.
- **Flight Search:** Search and explore available flights with travel dates and destinations.
- **Flexible Booking:** Supports One-way, Round-way, and Multi-city trips.
- **Passenger Management:** Select adults, children, and infants.
- **Seat Selection:** Choose between Economy, Premium, and Business classes.
- **Payment:** Multiple payment methods with voucher support.
- **Booking Confirmation:** Detailed confirmation with flight, seat, gate, terminal, and luggage information.
- **Rewards:** Points-based rewards system with airline vouchers and discounts.
- **Profile Management:** Personal information, travel history, documents, preferences, and settings.
- **Interactive Prototype:** Smart Animate, timed transitions, and interactive components.
